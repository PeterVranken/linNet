Four nodes and a branch in between all nodes to get a maximally complex
structure of the LES.

A result check can be done by looking first at the dependency of U~out~
only on U~1~ and then only on U~2~. For the dependency of U~out~ on U~1~,
when U~2~ is assumed zero, we can make these simplifications:

. U~1~ shortcuts R~5~; R~5~ has no impact on the transfer function.
. U~2~ shortcuts R~6~; R~6~ has no impact.
. Node 2 becomes same as node gnd.
. R~4~ is parallel to R~3~; the pair can be replaced by R~34~=R~3~*R~4~/(R~3~+R~4~)
. R~1~ is parallel to R~2~; the pair can be replaced by R~12~=R~1~*R~2~/(R~1~+R~2~)
. U~1~ feeds a voltage divide of R~34~ and R~12~; U~out~ is the negative voltage at R~12~
. U~out~=-R~12~/(R~12~+R~34~); this leads soon to linNets result:

----
                      -(R1*R2*R3 + R1*R2*R4)
Uout(U1) = --------------------------------------------- * U1
            (R1*R2*R3 + R1*R2*R4 + R1*R3*R4 + R2*R3*R4)
----

And analogously for Uout(U~2~).
