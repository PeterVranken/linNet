= Butterworth and Chebyshev

This folder contains several linNet test cases from publication
link:Versuch4-Aktiver-TP-OP.pdf["Aktiver Tiefpass mit Operationsverstärker"^],
downloaded as http://www.geoid.ch/misc/labor/Versuch4-Aktiver-TP-OP.pdf on
Mar 5^th^, 2014 (website no longer available). The publication proposes
different variants of a low pass filter of 4^th^ order, all of them made
from two consecutive stages of a basic
link:Versuch4-Aktiver-TP-OP-Fig-1.1.svg[op-amp filter of 2^nd^ order^].

== Chebyshev low pass filter

Here, we see the schematics of one of the resulting filters; it is a
Chebyshev low pass with a cutoff frequency of 10 kHz. The device values
are the same as in Figure 2.2 on page 8 of the original document:

[[figSchematics]]
.Chebyshev low pass, 4^th^ order, with standardized device values
image::Chebyshev-schematics-Fig-2.2.svg[Chebyshev low pass, width="80%", pdfwidth="80%", align="center"]

The other filter variants differ only in the chosen device values; their
otherwise identical schematics aren't shown here.

It is instructive to look first at the frequency responses of the two
stages, columns 3 and 4, and then to see how they combine to the designed
overall filter frequency response in column 2.

The link:Versuch4-Aktiver-TP-OP-Fig-1.1/Zi.svg[input impedance^] of the
filter is determined at the individual stage. The result of the numerical
calculation in Octave is displayed as a frequency response in dB. For low
frequencies, the values ​​are approximately 69 dB for the Chebyshev filter,
corresponding to 10^(69/20), which is the value of R~1~. Close to the
cutoff frequency, the impedance rises to its maximum of 6.5 kΩ.
